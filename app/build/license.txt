Voice models
------------
Character voice models in the built-in catalog are UNOFFICIAL community (fan-made)
RVC models hosted on Hugging Face. They are not included in this installer and are
downloaded only when you request them. Characters and their voice performances are
the property of their respective rights holders. Use the models for personal,
non-commercial entertainment only, and never to impersonate real people.

Virtual microphone
------------------
Routing audio into other applications requires a third-party virtual audio cable
driver such as VB-CABLE (https://vb-audio.com/Cable/), which is installed separately.
